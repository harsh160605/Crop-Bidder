How It Works

1. List Crops

Farmers list their crops with a base price.

2. Place Bids

Buyers place bids on the listed crops.

3. Sell Crops

Farmers choose the best bid & sell their crops.

Benefits

For Farmers

  • Get fair prices for your crops.
  • Reach a wider market of buyers.
  • Control when and to whom you sell.

For Buyers

  • Access high-quality crops directly from farmers.
  • Competitive bidding ensures the best prices.
  • Transparent and secure transactions.
